The plant

Profesor Aldo Lettura Matas Processing Plant

A beneficiation plant designed to deliver maximum efficiency in sulfide ore processing. Our circuit covers everything from control at ore receiving, through optimized crushing and grinding stages, to a selective flotation process that ensures the final quality of the concentrate.

01

Receiving and weighing

Truck reception, weighbridge weighing and sampling of each lot.

02

Crushing

Ore size reduction through primary and secondary crushing.

03

Grinding

Trapiche (Chilean) mills and ball mills grind the ore to the right particle size for the conditioner and then flotation.

04

Flotation

A bank of cells that separates the valuable minerals and turns them into concentrate.

05

Concentrate

Concentrate ready for sale.
