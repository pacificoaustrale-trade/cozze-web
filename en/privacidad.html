Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At COZZE MINERIA SpA we respect the privacy of the people who visit this website and contact us through it. This Policy explains what personal data we collect, what we use it for and how you can exercise your rights, in accordance with Chilean Law No. 19,628 on the Protection of Private Life and its amendments, including Law No. 21,719.

1. Data controller

  • Company name: COZZE MINERIA SpA
  • Tax ID (RUT): 78.110.699-2
  • Address: Ruta D-110 Km. 2.6, comuna de La Higuera, Región de Coquimbo, Chile
  • Contact email: contacto@cozze.cl

2. Data we collect

We only collect the data you voluntarily provide through the contact form:

  • Full name
  • Company (optional)
  • Phone (optional)
  • Email address
  • Profile (for example, producer or buyer) and the content of your message

We do not request sensitive data. Please do not include it in your message.

In addition, as with any website, the hosting provider automatically records technical data about the visit (such as IP address, browser type and date and time of access) for security purposes and to operate the service.

3. Purpose of processing

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests for information.
  • Assess and manage requests for toll processing, ore purchase or sale of concentrates.
  • Maintain communications related to your request or to an existing business relationship.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we will not send you advertising without your prior consent.

4. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the acceptance box on the form, and, where applicable, to carry out pre-contractual or contractual measures that you request. You may withdraw your consent at any time, without retroactive effect.

5. Disclosure of data to third parties

We do not share your data with third parties, except:

  • Technology providers that supply services necessary to operate this website (for example, web hosting), acting on our behalf and solely for that purpose. Some of them may store data on servers located outside Chile, applying adequate security measures.
  • Public authorities, when there is a legal obligation or a court order.

6. Retention period

We keep your data for as long as necessary to handle your inquiry and, at most, up to 24 months from our last contact, unless there is an ongoing business relationship or a legal obligation (for example, tax-related) that requires us to keep it longer. Once this period has ended, we delete or anonymize it.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against loss, unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. Access to it is restricted to the staff who need it to handle your request.

8. Your rights

As the data subject, you may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access: to know what data about you we process.
  • Rectification: to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: to request that we delete your data.
  • Objection: to object to a specific processing.
  • Portability: to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Blocking: to request the temporary suspension of processing.

To exercise them, write to contacto@cozze.cl with the subject “Personal data”, stating your name, the right you wish to exercise and a means for us to reply to you. We may ask you for documentation to verify your identity. We will respond within the time limits established by law.

If you believe that we have not properly handled your request, you may refer the matter to the Chilean Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales) or to the competent courts.

9. Cookies

This website does not use its own tracking or advertising cookies. To display the site’s typography, fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may mean that your browser shares technical data (such as your IP address) with Google. Links to Google Maps are governed by Google’s privacy policies.

10. Minors

This website is intended for mining producers, companies and buyers. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18 years of age.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect legal changes or changes to our services. We will publish the current version on this page, indicating the date of its last update.
